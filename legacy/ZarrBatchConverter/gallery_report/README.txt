Gallery report generator (16 Oct 2026).
1. charts.py <this folder>   draws chart_*.png into fig\ from synapse_summary.csv, image_list_images.csv and the comparison CSVs
2. build_report.py [out.pdf]  builds the PDF (default: Desktop\pan-ExM 2026 Gallery Report.pdf)
synapse_summary.csv is the Summary sheet of Synapse Log.xlsx; image_list_images.csv the Images sheet of X:\...\Zarr Image List.xlsx.
fig\*.jpg are downscaled copies of gallery figures; re-copy them when the sources change.
3. diagram.py <this folder>   draws fig\pipeline.png and figxf_schematic.png
4. build_setup_doc.py [out.pdf]  builds Desktop\pan-ExM Analysis Setup.pdf (the toolchain document)
